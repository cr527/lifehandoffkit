# Life Handoff Kit Site Refresh

Working repo for improving the Life Handoff Kit landing page at https://lifehandoffkit.com/.

## Current Goal

Turn the page from a mostly text-led concept page into a more tangible product page with stronger visual proof, clearer conversion paths, and a more premium "calm healthcare software" feel.

## Critique Themes To Address

- Add stronger product proof above the fold.
- Make the Readiness Score dashboard a primary sales asset.
- Add a "what you get" thumbnail strip with concrete previews.
- Reduce text density in favor of scannable sections.
- Make the Critical 5 feel like a system, not a list.
- Bring privacy and local-first trust language closer to the purchase CTA.
- Replace `mailto:` launch/checklist links with real checkout and lead capture when those URLs are available.

## Proposed Homepage Order

1. Hero with 60-minute/80-percent-ready promise and product collage.
2. Trust strip: PDF, Sheets dashboard, scripts, local-first privacy.
3. What you get: thumbnail previews of the Emergency Summary, Critical 5, dashboard, scripts, document locations, quarterly check-in.
4. Problem section: what families usually discover too late.
5. Dashboard feature section with large readiness score preview.
6. Critical 5 visual system.
7. Privacy section: no portal, no password storage, your storage choice.
8. Free checklist CTA.
9. Complete kit CTA.
10. FAQ.
